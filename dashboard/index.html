How the ranking works

Every protocol in the snapshot that records no token is scored. The model never uses a hardcoded list of names: each category's FDV/TVL multiple is calibrated from the snapshot itself, as the median market-cap/TVL ratio of that category's already-tokenised protocols (categories with fewer than 3 tokenised peers fall back to the global median, ).

Implied FDV = TVL × category multiple × momentum (7-day TVL change, clamped, worth ×0.70–×1.30) × age (longer tokenless-and-large is worth up to ×1.25) × a size decay for very large TVL. Implied value = implied FDV × 7.5% assumed airdrop share × P(launch), where P(launch) comes from the protocol's TVL tier, its tokenless age, its category's tokenisation rate and the sign of its momentum. Rows are ranked by implied value, highest first.
